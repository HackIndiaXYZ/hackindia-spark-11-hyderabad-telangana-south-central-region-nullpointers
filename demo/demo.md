# CROWDOS // 5-Minute Hackathon Presentation Script

This script coordinates your presentation narrative with the CROWDOS UI states to create a high-fidelity, high-emotion live demo that highlights CROWDOS's predictive operational intelligence.

---

### [00:00 - 00:20] Landing Screen // The Hook
- **Visual**: Show the dark, cinematic Landing Page with glowing nodes and the main CTA: `INITIALIZE SYSTEM`.
- **Narrative**: 
  > *"Judges, every year millions of people gather at high-density public venues like stadiums and transit plazas. Operators currently rely on fragmented tools: security radios, isolated CCTV feeds, and spreadsheets. When a crisis hits, response is slow and fragmented. Introducing CROWDOS: Operational Intelligence for High-Density Events. Let's enter the command center."*
- **Action**: Click `INITIALIZE SYSTEM` to animate the Role Selector Grid.

---

### [00:20 - 00:40] Role Selection // System Stationing
- **Visual**: The 5 station roles grid lights up. Hover over them to show dynamic border glow states.
- **Narrative**:
  > *"CROWDOS is customized for the user role of the operator. Whether you are the Transport Manager, the Medical Officer, or the global Operations Commander, the system re-aligns your widgets, Digital Twin highlights, and priority alerts. Today we will log in as the Operations Commander."*
- **Action**: Click the `Operations Commander` card to enter the Dashboard.

---

### [00:40 - 01:10] Nominal Operations // The Digital Twin
- **Visual**: The Dashboard loads. Ticker reads `NOMINAL`, **Operational Health** pulses green at `94%`, and the SVG Digital Twin shows green sectors, open green gates, and circulating security/volunteer patrols (moving dots).
- **Narrative**:
  > *"We are inside our FIFA World Cup command deck. Here, the SVG Digital Twin is the hero. It tracks perimeter gate queues, sector seating densities, and active field units in real-time. Everything is nominal. Operational Health stands at 94%."*
- **Action**: Click and point to the different sectors on the map and active patrol dots.

---

### [01:10 - 02:00] Crisis Ingestion // The Threat Cascade
- **Visual**: Select the **Heavy Rain Deluge** scenario from the sidebar (or let Mission Control drive it). The stadium weather overlay turns into diagonal falling rain. Operational Health drops to `78%`, and Sector North/South turn orange/red.
- **Narrative**:
  > *"Now, let's trigger a crisis: Heavy Rain. Watch the Digital Twin instantly react: a diagonal rain canvas overlays the stadium. Spectators immediately crowd the covered concourse zones B & C to escape the rain. Crowd density spikes. Because of the wet stairs, we ingest multiple medical slips. Global Operational Health collapses to 78%."*
- **Action**: Point out the red pulsing Sector B and the active warning alert boxes.

---

### [02:00 - 03:00] Decision Intelligence // System Mitigation
- **Visual**: Point to the **Decision Intelligence Console** (NASA Console) showing: `[STATUS: CAUTION]` and generating the mitigation briefing and directive: *"Deploy Shelter & Reroute Flow"*.
- **Narrative**:
  > *"Instead of leaving operators to panic, CROWDOS Decision Intelligence analyzes the telemetry flow. It estimates a 92% confidence score and suggests a mitigation protocol: Deploy emergency shelters at uncovered gates, and redirect 30% of Gate B queue to Gate C corridor. Let's execute."*
- **Action**: Click `APPROVE RECOMMENDATION`.

---

### [03:00 - 04:00] Action Approval // NASA Command Pipeline
- **Visual**: The console initiates the sequential blink logs: `[CMD] RECOMMENDATION ACCEPTED` -> `[OPS] VOLUNTEER DEPLOYMENT SEQUENCE ENGAGED`... The Digital Twin updates: gate occupancies relax, ambulance responders move, and Operational Health rises back to `91%` (Nominal).
- **Narrative**:
  > *"When I approve the recommendation, a sequential command pipeline executes directly on screen. Volunteer teams deploy, gate traffic is redistributed, and you can see the Digital Twin zones return to a safe green status. Operational Health recovers to 91%."*
- **Action**: Scroll down to the **Decision Replay** panel.

---

### [04:00 - 04:45] Decision Replay // Counterfactual Sandbox
- **Visual**: Click on the logged directive entry in the timeline to expand it. Show the side-by-side **Actual Intervention** (91% health, green) vs. **No Intervention** (45% health, red pulsing).
- **Narrative**:
  > *"To evaluate performance, we open the Decision Replay. We don't just log what we did. We run a counterfactual simulator showing a 'What-If' comparison. Without our intervention, the platform calculates that the stands density would have caused severe crushing hazards at Gate B, collapsing Operational Health to 45%. CROWDOS prevented a catastrophe."*
- **Action**: Show the side-by-side panel metrics.

---

### [04:45 - 05:00] Wrap Up // Extensibility
- **Visual**: Toggle another scenario (e.g., Gate Scanner Failure) or show the Mission Control Auto-Runner executing.
- **Narrative**:
  > *"CROWDOS is venue-agnostic and extensible to any high-density venue. One single number—Operational Health—governs the command. This is how we transform fragmented logs into unified, operational intelligence. Thank you."*
